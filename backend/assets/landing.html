How it works

  2. Show your screen

    Share your iPhone screen, so OjO sees what you see.

Your data

This form keeps your email and the time you asked, and nothing else. We use them only to write to you about the beta.

On a call, your screen, only while you share it, and your voice go to our server and Meta's AI. What OjO says goes to ElevenLabs for the voice. While we test, we keep the words and screens from calls to fix problems. Payment screens we recognize aren't kept.
